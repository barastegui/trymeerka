Last updated: November 2026

Meerka is an independent mobile application for travelers. It is not operated by a registered company.

Meerka does not collect, store, or process any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information.

6. Children's privacy

Meerka is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.
